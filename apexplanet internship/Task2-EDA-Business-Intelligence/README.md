# Netflix EDA & Business Intelligence Project

## ApexPlanet Data Analytics Internship - Task 2

### Project Overview

This project focuses on Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) and Business Intelligence using the Netflix Movies and TV Shows dataset. The objective is to analyze the dataset, identify patterns, answer business questions using SQL, and create visualizations to support data-driven insights.

---

## Dataset Information

* Dataset: Netflix Movies and TV Shows
* Total Records: 8807
* Total Columns: 12

### Features

* show_id
* type
* title
* director
* cast
* country
* date_added
* release_year
* rating
* duration
* listed_in
* description

---

## Objectives

## Key Findings

### Content Type Analysis

* Movies: 6131
* TV Shows: 2676
* Movies account for approximately 69.6% of Netflix content.

### Country Analysis

* United States: 2818 titles
* India: 972 titles
* United Kingdom: 419 titles

### Rating Analysis

* TV-MA is the most common rating.
* TV-14 is the second most common rating.

### Release Year Analysis

* Content production peaked in 2018.
* Significant growth was observed between 2012 and 2018.

### Genre Analysis

* Dramas and International Movies dominate the catalog.
* Documentaries and Stand-Up Comedy are also highly represented.

## Business Insights

1. Movies dominate Netflix's content library.
2. The United States and India are the leading content producers.
3. Netflix primarily targets mature audiences through TV-MA and TV-14 content.
4. Content production reached its highest level in 2018.
5. Drama and International Movies are the most represented genres.
